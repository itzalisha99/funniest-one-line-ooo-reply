An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty "Funniest One-Line Out-of-Office Reply".

## The line

> I am out of the office until Tuesday. I left detailed instructions for my replacement: me, but on Monday, and much less cheerful.

The joke is the handover: the replacement is yourself, one day earlier and already less cheerful — a real out-of-office message with the punch landing in the last words.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and checks it first against the bounty gate (1–160 characters, one line, not only white space) with a `validate()` function.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
